```
JellyGlass 2.8.0

• Givre, le thème de Noël : jusqu'au 6 janvier, bleu nuit, titres gravés, givre aux coins de l'écran et neige qui tombe. À activer depuis « Quoi de neuf » ou Réglages → Apparence
```

## English

```
JellyGlass 2.8.0

• Frost, the Christmas theme: until January 6, night blue, engraved titles, frost in the corners of the screen and falling snow. Turn it on from "What's new" or Settings → Appearance
• On Home, a "Family Christmas" row
• A festive icon for December
• Your languages follow the show: the audio and subtitles you pick stay for the whole show, and a film you resume opens in the same languages, on all your devices
• Set your preferred languages in Settings → Playback → Languages
• Subtitles in a separate file (.srt) show up without the server having to convert the video
• See it before you ask*: a title to request opens on its own page, with picture, summary, cast and details
• After a dropped connection, playback picks up where it stopped
• Bug fixes

* Requires a Seerr server

Thanks for using JellyGlass. Bug reports and suggestions: https://github.com/b-raillard/Cinemax/issues
```
