## HomeScreen

**RULE — a Home rail the user has switched off is NOT fetched** (`HomeRailPreferences` in `SettingsKeys.swift` → the `rails` snapshot read once at the top of `HomeViewModel.load`). Every branch used to run unconditionally, so « Prochainement » and « Collections » — both **off by default** — cost two requests on every Home load of every default install, and switching a rail off in Settings saved nothing at all. Gated: next-up, favourites, playlists, upcoming, collections, « Parce que vous avez vu » (its seed query AND its similar fetch, at load and on tier-2), the genre fan-out (up to 8 queries, the most expensive thing on the screen) and the « En direct » pair. **Continue Watching and Recently Added are deliberately NOT gated**: those two fetches also feed `heroItem` (`resumeItems.first ?? latestItems.first`) and the hero is never gated, so skipping them would blank the top of the screen. `HomeRailPreferences.isOn` falls back to the `SettingsKey.Default` value for an **absent** key — a bare `UserDefaults.bool(forKey:)` reads absent as `false`, which on a fresh install would switch off every rail the app had never been told about. **A rail switched back ON is filled by `HomeViewModel.refreshRail(_:using:)`**, raised from ONE `HomeScreen.onChange(of: enabledRails)` (a `Set<Rail>`; `now.subtracting(was)` is what just appeared) — seven `onChange(of: Bool)` modifiers on that body defeat the type-checker outright. Never a reload: that flips `isLoading` and costs the scroll position. Locked by `HomeRailGatingTests`.

**RULE — « Reprendre » asks for `mediaTypes: [.video]`, and that parameter is load-bearing on Jellyfin 12.0** (`JellyfinAPIClient.getResumeItems`, #209). Without it, with the app's exact query shape, `GET /UserItems/Resume` answers on 12.0 with the **Season and the Series** of a part-watched episode alongside the episode itself — measured 2026-09-12 against throwaway servers: 10.10.7 → `movie, episode`; 10.11.11 → `movie, episode`; **12.0.0 → `movie, episode, Season 1, the Series`**, the last two at position 0. Home renders that list VERBATIM, so the rail drew two cards with no media source behind them, and `heroItem` (`resumeItems.first ?? latestItems.first`) could make one of them the hero of the screen. The mechanism is visible in the 12.0 fixture: those two entries carry `MediaType: "Unknown"` where both playable ones carry `"Video"`. **Chosen over `includeItemTypes: [.movie, .episode]` and `excludeItemTypes: [.season, .series]` — all three were measured and all three fix 12.0** — because the Widget's hand-built copy of this route (`JellyfinLite.fetchResumeItems`) **already sends `mediaTypes=Video`**, so this makes the app agree with its own extension instead of adding a second spelling of one rule, and because "playable video" admits a future playable kind that an allow-list would silently drop. It also closes a worse path than the cosmetic one: `PrivacySecurityScreen.clearContinueWatching` calls `markItemUnplayed` on **every** entry this query returns, and a series-level unplayed toggle **cascades to every episode** (see the `episodes-` cache RULE), so on 12.0 « Effacer Reprendre » was about to wipe a whole series' watched history rather than one resume point. Non-regressive on 10.x, measured: all four candidate shapes return the same two playable items there. Locked by `ServerDTODecodingFixtureTests`.

`HomeViewModel.load` fans out a parallel `TaskGroup`: `resumeItems` (limit 20), `latestItems`, `favoriteItems` (movie/series, dateCreated desc, limit 20), `nextUpItems` (`getNextUpEpisodes` limit 20). **RULE — `latestItems` ("Ajouts récents") has TWO sources, merged by `HomeViewModel.mergeRecentlyAdded`, and is NOT a bare `getLatestMedia`/`/Items/Latest`.** That endpoint scans raw items with episodes included and groups them under their series (`groupItems` defaults true), so a library ingesting one show's back catalogue fills the entire scan with its episodes and they collapse into a **single** card — the row looks empty while faithfully reporting that the newest N items all belong to one series (observed with a full Arrow import). But that grouping is also the *feature*: it's what resurfaces a long-owned series when a new season lands. So: (1) `getItems` over `[.movie, .series]` sorted `dateCreated` desc for new titles, (2) `getSeriesWithRecentEpisodes` (grouped `/Items/Latest` over `[.episode]`, filtered to `.series`) for shows that just got episodes. **Each source has its own budget** (`recentlyAddedLimit` 20 / `newEpisodeShowsLimit` 6), so no single show can crowd the row out — and that holds regardless of whether the server applies its limit before or after grouping, which is not verifiable from the client. **The union cannot be date-sorted**: a grouped series carries its OWN creation date, not the date of the episode that surfaced it, so sorting would bury exactly the shows source (2) exists to reveal — hence shows-with-new-episodes lead, capped, and new titles fill the rest. **The two sources are fetched CONCURRENTLY (two `async let`, app and widget)**: this branch feeds `heroItem`, so it sits on the first-display path — 184 ms in series vs 128 ms in parallel against the NAS (lot 8, audit P6). Sources fail independently (one erroring still populates the row); id-less items are shown, not silently dropped. **The Widget mirrors this** in its own dependency-free copy (`JellyfinLite.fetchSeriesWithRecentEpisodes` + `PosterRailProvider.loadRecentlyAdded`), capped to a third of its 3-or-7-poster grid. Note the widget was never exposed to the crowding half — it already sent `includeItemTypes=Movie,Series` — but it had the same blind spot for new seasons. Locked by `HomeViewModelTests` (the widget links no test target). `heroItem = resumeItems.first ?? latestItems.first`; `hasLoaded` guard prevents tab-remount refetch (`reload` bypasses). **RULE — progressive render**: `isLoading` flips false right after this phase-1 group + `heroItem` (NOT at the end of `load`), so the hero + rails paint while genre rows and the episode-nav maps keep filling in off their own `@Observable` slices (nav maps only gate the in-player prev/next buttons). A separate `isFullyLoaded` flips true only at the very end; `HomeScreen` gates the all-empty `EmptyStateView` behind `isFullyLoaded` (`isLoading || (isHomeEmpty && !isFullyLoaded) ⇒ skeleton`) so the empty state can NEVER flash mid-load while later phases populate — don't collapse the two flags back into one. Episode nav (BOTH the Continue Watching + Next Up rails): `fetchSeasonEpisodes` fetches each referenced season's episode list once, **deduped across BOTH rails** (union of `resumeItems + nextUpItems` seasons — a season referenced by both is fetched a single time; the `getEpisodes` 10s cache backs this up too) and **chunked at 6 concurrent** like every other fan-out here (the two rails reference up to 40 seasons and this runs alongside the genre fan-out), then the pure `buildNavigationMap(seasonEpisodes:)` derives each map from the shared dict — `precomputeEpisodeRefs` once/season, O(1) `buildEpisodeNavigation` → `resumeNavigation` / `nextUpNavigation` (`[String:(prev,next,navigator)]`). Both `continueWatchingPlayLink` and `nextUpPlayLink` thread these into `PlayLink` — the player's prev/next episode buttons are gated on non-nil `previousEpisode`/`nextEpisode` (`VLCStreamPresenter` tvOS `controlBar` / iOS `transportRow`), so a rail that omits them shows no episode-nav buttons. Within-season only (mirrors resume; a season-opener Next Up gets `next` but no `prev`). **RULE — « En direct » is ONE row fed by TWO sources with TWO different permissions** (`LiveSessionsRow.build`, pure + locked by `LiveSessionsRowTests`). A SyncPlay group and `/Sessions` describe the same people from two angles: Marie and Paul watching together are *one* group and *two* sessions on the same title, so a separate "Séances en cours" row would have stated the same fact twice, three lines apart — which reads as a bug, not as richness. Folding a group into a single card also fixes something the row already got wrong alone: three people on one film used to draw three identical cards. **Both halves are governed PER USER, and neither is `isAdministrator` any more**: `GET /SyncPlay/List` by the server's own `UserPolicy.syncPlayAccess` (`canJoin`), `/Sessions` by `canSeeOthers` — see the two admin RULEs above for why the second is `enableRemoteControlOfOtherUsers` and what that couples. Each half is fetched only when its own permission allows it and neither is ever faked in the other's absence, so an account with groups but no sessions gets a row of joinable cards with no artwork rather than a row of blanks. The Settings toggle shows when *either* half is reachable. A group card whose artwork is unknown draws `person.2.fill` (`WideCard.fallbackIcon`), never the default `play.rectangle` — having no artwork is a group's ORDINARY state, and the "missing video" glyph made it read as a broken card. **A group's title can legitimately be `nil`**: `GroupInfoDto` carries `{GroupId, GroupName, State, Participants, LastUpdatedAt}` and **no item at all**, so what a group is watching is unknowable from that endpoint — artwork is borrowed from a participant's session when `/Sessions` is visible, and otherwise **the card falls back to the group's NAME**, which is the one thing about a session that reaches every member. That is why `WatchTogetherSheet.defaultGroupName` now seeds the name with the WORK'S TITLE rather than « Groupe de Bastien »: it costs no request and no permission, and it is the whole of what a card can say to someone who may not read `/Sessions`. A blank name stays `nil` (the view substitutes « Séance en cours »; an empty string would sail past that and draw a headless card). **A card whose item is an EPISODE prints the same « S01:E02 - Titre » line the « À suivre » / « Reprendre » rails do** (`Entry.episodeLabel`, from the shared `BaseItemDto.episodeLabel` helper in `BaseItemDto+Metadata.swift` — the SSOT for that string, which used to be hand-built in three places): `title` names the SERIES, so without it the row said « Xavier regarde Marvel's The Punisher » and never which episode. On a solo card the episode takes the subtitle and « Xavier regarde » sits on the `detail` line — **for a film too, whose subtitle line stays BLANK**: every card of the row passes `WideCard(reservesTextLines: true)`, which draws a hidden placeholder for an absent subtitle / detail, because cards printing a different number of lines did not merely misalign their text, the one carrying the extra line had its artwork squeezed out of 16:9 (640 × 300 beside 640 × 360 on an Apple TV, 2026-09-16); on a group card the participants keep the subtitle and the episode LEADS the `detail` line, ahead of state and age, so truncation costs the age, not the episode. Locked by `LiveSessionsRowTests.episodeLabel`. `State` and `LastUpdatedAt` were parsed and then dropped at render time; they now feed `WideCard`'s third `detail:` line (« en pause · ouverte il y a 4 min ») — `.idle` is not rendered (it reads as a fault rather than as "nobody has started yet"), an unknown state string is dropped rather than guessed, and `minutesSinceUpdate` clamps at 0 so a client clock ahead of the server's cannot report a session opened in the future. Joining routes the resulting `PlayQueue` through `SyncPlayController.onQueueChanged` → the same `pendingIntentPlaybackItemId` + `pendingDeepLinkItemId` pair an App Intent and an inbound « Lire sur… » use, so a join inherits the fidelity of a tap — series → next-up, version pick, prev/next. **RULE — the START POSITION is the one thing a join does NOT inherit: the GROUP's position is authoritative, and a group at 0 beats a local resume** (`SyncPlayJoinStart`, locked by `SyncPlayJoinAndReadyTests`; the group's ticks travel as `pendingIntentPlaybackStartTicks`). This sentence used to say "resume position" among the inherited fidelities, and that was **wrong, not a trade-off**: the reasoning was imported from « Lire sur… », where re-resolving locally *"lands on the same tick for this app's own sender"* — definitionally false here, since the sender is a **different account with a different resume**. `onQueueChanged = { itemId, _ in … }` discarded the position and the request fell through to `resolvedPlayTarget`, i.e. the JOINING account's `playbackPositionTicks`. Measured on device 2026-09-06: group at 3:39, joiner opened on the end credits at 12:33, gap never corrected, no signal — then the joiner "finished" a film the group had just started and wiped its own resume. Note `nil` and `0` are **not** interchangeable here: a group starting from the beginning carries `0`, and treating that as "no position given" is precisely what let the 12:33 resume through. Symmetrically, **a group created on a half-watched item seeds its queue with the CREATOR's resume**, never a hardcoded `0` (`SyncPlayJoinStart.queueStartTicks`, threaded through `WatchTogetherIntent.startTicks` → `WatchTogetherSheet.startPositionTicks` → `setQueue`): starting at 0 restarted the film under the host and then overwrote their resume server-side — « 4m restantes » became « 12m restantes » — silent data loss from an action whose purpose is not to touch the resume point. The **solo** card deliberately carries no "watch with them" action — see the STATUS note: Jellyfin has no invitation primitive. Configurable layout (`home.show*`, default true; hero never gated). Empty state in `ScrollView` (pull-to-refresh works). **tvOS scroll-to-top on reappearance**: `ScrollViewReader` + `.id("home.top")` attached *directly to `heroSection`* (fallback `Color.clear.frame(height: 0)` only when no hero) + `.onAppear` `scrollTo`. MovieLibraryScreen/SearchScreen/Settings tvOS landing keep the separate zero-height sentinel because their first visible row already sits flush against the safe-area top — see tab-bar pill RULE below.

**RULE — an unreachable server is NOT an empty library** (`HomeViewModel.loadFailure`, audit 2026-09-22, U1). When EVERY phase-1 source fails, `load()` records the first error and returns before phase 2 (whose genre fan-out would only fail the same way, after another request timeout behind the skeleton); `HomeScreen` then shows `homeErrorState` — `ErrorStateView` with `userFacingMessage`, `.offline` only when that message is `error.network` — instead of « Votre bibliothèque est vide », which told an offline user their content was gone. Only while nothing is on screen: a failed refresh never blanks rails that loaded earlier. The tier-2 refresh re-derives `heroItem` from the refreshed resume rail (tvOS renders `heroItem`, and `load()` was its only writer — B7), gates Next Up / Favorites on `HomeRailPreferences` like `load()`, and equality-guards every rail write (P4).

**RULE — `HomeViewModel.load` carries a `loadGeneration` and clears the rails when the server / account changes** (`loadedIdentity` = `activeServerId|userId`, `clearContent()`; audit 2026-09-22, B13). Phase-1 results are collected, then applied only if no newer load started; phase 2 re-checks before the nav maps and before `isFullyLoaded`. **Every writer of a rail slice checks `isCurrent(generation)` — generation AND `!Task.isCancelled`** (`loadGenreRows`, `loadActiveSessions`, `loadBecauseYouWatched`, `retryGenre`; the refreshers pass the generation current when they start): returning from a superseded load cancels its `async let` children, whose `try?` then come back empty, and written out they painted `.failed` genre rows and an empty « En direct » over the newer load's screen — hiding its error state, since `isHomeEmpty` counts genre rows. Without the identity clear, the previous server's rails stayed on screen — tappable, naming ids this server does not have — for the whole new load, and for good when it failed, since `loadFailure` only replaces an EMPTY Home. Rail switches and genre picks are read from an injected `UserDefaults` (`HomeViewModel(defaults:)`, `HomeRailPreferences.snapshot(in:)`, `HomeGenrePreferences.*(in:)`), which is what lets the test suites stop sharing `.standard` (T2).

- **Genre rows are user-configurable + deterministic (de-randomization decision — no more shuffle/random)**: `getGenres` → sorted `localizedCaseInsensitiveCompare` (matches the Settings picker order) → `HomeGenrePreferences.effectiveGenres(available:)` — unconfigured ⇒ deterministic first-6 prefix; configured ⇒ explicit picks re-ordered to canonical order, **no cap**. Row items are `getItems` sorted `.dateCreated` **descending** (drops the costly server `ORDER BY RANDOM()`). Fan-out chunked at 6 concurrent (spares self-hosted servers). **Failures become `.failed`** (retry chip); empty success drops the row. Live refresh: `.onChange` of the `home.selectedGenres` `@AppStorage` string → `reloadGenreRows`.
- **Playlists rail** (`home.showPlaylists`, next to Favorites — both are sets the user assembled): `getPlaylists` in the same parallel `TaskGroup` as the other rails, each source failing on its own. **This rail is why playlists are findable at all**: before it, `getPlaylists` had exactly ONE caller in the whole app — the "add to a playlist" sheet — and the only screen that listed playlists needed a `.custom + .library` menu *and* a server exposing a Playlists view, so on the default five tabs a created playlist could never be seen again. Cards drill into `PlaylistDetailScreen` and deliberately carry **no** `mediaCardContextMenu`: a playlist is a folder, and none of that menu's entries (play, watched, favorite, add to a playlist) mean anything on one — same reason `LibraryFolderBrowseScreen` has none. Locked by `HomePlaylistsRailTests`.
- **« Parce que vous avez vu {titre} » rail** (`home.showBecauseYouWatched`, `HomeViewModel.loadBecauseYouWatched`, #170): seeded by the most recently played item — `getItems([.movie, .episode], filters: [.isPlayed], sortBy: [.datePlayed] desc, limit: 1, enableTotalRecordCount: false)` — and filled by `getSimilarItems(limit: becauseYouWatchedLimit)` (20). **An episode seeds its SERIES** (`becauseYouWatchedSeed`): "because you watched S01E04" names nothing, and `/Similar` on an episode answers with episodes. **Exclusions** (`becauseYouWatchedItems`, pure + `nonisolated` like `mergeRecentlyAdded`): the seed, anything `userData.isPlayed`, anything in Continue Watching **and the series of a resumed episode** (a show you are halfway through is not a recommendation), id-less items and duplicates. `nil` hides the rail — no played item, or nothing left after filtering — so there is never a header over nothing. **It loads in PHASE 2** (beside the genre fan-out, never in the phase-1 group): the exclusion list is `resumeItems`, only known once phase 1 lands, and the row sits just above the genre rows so filling in late shifts nothing already painted. **It re-seeds on tier-2** (`refreshUserDataRails`, gated on the switch): finishing a film is exactly when the seed should change. Writes go through the equality-guarded `setBecauseYouWatched` (tier-2 re-runs it on every watched toggle, and an identical write still invalidates the tvOS cards). A failed request leaves the rail as it was; a successful empty answer hides it. **Known staleness, accepted**: `similar-` is a 5-min cache outside `userDataCachePrefixes`, so a title marked watched FROM the rail can linger until that expires or the seed moves. Cards are `recentlyAddedCard` (poster/300/tag — prefetched byte-identically — status overlay, shared context menu). Locked by `HomeRailGatingTests`.
- **Next Up rail** (`home.showNextUp`): global next-unwatched episode per in-progress series — distinct from resume points; cards play from the start (no resume offset). Title = `seriesName`, subtitle `SxxExx - name`. Cards get prev/next episode buttons in the player via `nextUpNavigation` (see Episode nav above).
- **RULE — iOS hero carousel; tvOS keeps the single static hero**: `heroCandidates` = up to 5 distinct from `resumeItems + latestItems`; entries after the first REQUIRE `hasBackdropImage` (crossfade must never flash the fallback). Auto-advance every 8s (`runHeroRotation`, `.easeInOut(0.6)` crossfade, `.task(id:)`-scoped so it cancels on disappear) — **no-op unless `motionEffects` is on AND ≥2 candidates**. Swipe via `simultaneousGesture` `DragGesture` (direction + 50pt threshold). Dots: accent active / `onSurfaceVariant.opacity(0.4)`, centered, `allowsHitTesting(false)`, and — for VoiceOver — ONE adjustable element (« À la une, 2 sur 5 », swipe up/down pages; lot 9). **RULE — never page the carousel with `accessibilityScrollAction` on the hero**: it also takes the vertical three-finger swipe that scrolls Home; `currentHeroIndex` clamps against a shrinking set. tvOS is deliberately excluded — auto-advancing focusable chrome fights the focus engine / `.focusSection()` machinery.
- **Continue Watching context menu**: long-press (iOS) / long-press-select (tvOS), attached to the PlayLink button not the label (preserves focus). Actions: **Mark as watched** (`markItemPlayed`) and **Remove** (`markItemUnplayed`). **RULE — Jellyfin has no hide-from-resume endpoint** — clearing played state resets the resume position, which is what drops it from `/UserItems/Resume`. Both act optimistically (remove card, toast, post the **tier-2 `.cinemaxItemUserDataChanged`**), restore-on-failure with clamped index. `mutateResumeItem` does NOT call `refreshResume` itself — Home's own tier-2 handler runs `refreshUserDataRails` (resume fetched exactly once); see the two-tier refresh RULE.
- **FavoritesScreen** ("watchlist"): aggregated hearted movies/series — `getItems` `isFavorite`, sorted `.sortName` **ascending**, paginated 40/page via `PaginatedLoader` (deliberately different from the Home Favorites row: dateCreated-desc/20, one-shot). `LazyVGrid` (tvOS 6 cols; iOS `AdaptiveLayout`); reloads on `.cinemaxShouldRefreshCatalogue` (full reset — an explicit refresh) and **refreshes the loaded span** on `.cinemaxFavoritesChanged` + tier-2 `.cinemaxItemUserDataChanged`. **RULE — those two go through `PaginatedLoader.refreshLoadedSpan`, never `load()`**: this grid's own cards raise them from their context menu, and `load()`'s `loader.reset()` collapsed a deeply-scrolled list to one page **under the user's finger**. `refreshLoadedSpan` re-requests everything already paged in as ONE request from index 0, preserving item identity (hence scroll position and per-card `@State`) and re-deriving `hasLoadedAll` from the fresh total so a shrunken set stops asking for more. `WatchedHistoryScreen` follows the same split. **TWO entries** (lot 9): the Home Favorites row "View All" (`showViewAll: true` → `favoritesDestination` token → screen-root `navigationDestination(item:)`, `.tvPushedScreen()` applied AT THAT PUSH SITE) and **Réglages → Compte → Favoris** (`FavoritesSheet` = `NavigationStack { FavoritesScreen(isModal: true) }`, iOS toolbar Done / tvOS header Done + `onExitCommand` on the stack's ROOT content, same chrome as `WatchedHistoryScreen`). Home used to be the only door, so a hidden Favoris row or a custom menu without Accueil made the screen unreachable. Not a tab; declares no own `NavigationStack`. **RULE — never mark `FavoritesScreen` itself `tvPushedScreen()`** (it is also a modal root), and never give its PUSHED form an `onExitCommand` — a `nil` handler backgrounds the app, an ancestor one swallows the fiche's pop.

- **RULE — tvOS 26 tab bar pill alignment heuristic**: Liquid Glass reserves a constant 157pt `safeAreaInsets.top` container above the scroll content (verified by `GeometryReader` — identical on every tab), but the active pill aligns to either the **bottom** of that container ("expanded", default) or the **top** ("compact", ~30pt higher visually) based on whether the first scrollable row touches the safe-area top edge. Any leading gap — including the implicit `LazyVStack` `spacing:` between a zero-height sentinel and the first visible row — flips the pill into compact mode, making the menu look like it shifted upward on that tab (and that tab only). Fix pattern: `LazyVStack(spacing: 0)` + explicit `.padding(.bottom, spacing6)` between rows + attach the scroll-anchor `.id(scrollTopID)` directly to the first visible row instead of a separate sentinel. Reference: `HomeScreen.content` (the hero carries `.id(scrollTopID)` and the LazyVStack uses `spacing: 0`). Films/Recherche/Réglages don't need the workaround because their first row (`tvTopBar` / `searchField` / `tvLandingPage`) already sits flush — they have `LazyVStack(spacing: 0)` or use eager VStacks. Diagnosis is non-trivial because content-level padding, focusable proximity, `toolbarBackgroundVisibility`, and body-level modal modifiers all have **no effect** on the pill alignment — only the gap above the first scroll row matters.
